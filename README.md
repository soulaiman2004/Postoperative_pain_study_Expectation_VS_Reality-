## ABOUT THE STUDY
The study took place between late March and May 2026. Collected baseline data included demographics, patients' psychological status, sleep quality on the night before surgery, height (measured by two researchers), weight (also measured), and full medical history.

Operative time was measured from the first incision to the last suture. Preoperative pain expectations were assessed using the Numerical Rating Scale (NRS).

Patients were re-evaluated within the first 24 hours postoperatively (at roughly 6:00 PM on the day of surgery for same-day discharges, and at 9:00 AM the next day for those discharged later). During this follow-up, average, maximum, and minimum postoperative pain—as well as pain upon coughing and movement were recorded using the NRS. Informed consent was obtained from all participants prior to enrollment.
